Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026  ·  Developer: Rafz Services

We keep it simple: Hit Them Hard does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information. The data described below is used solely to serve ads and process purchases.

Advertising — Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. When the app launches, you will be asked whether to allow tracking. Your choice controls whether ads are personalised.

Regardless of your choice, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data

If you allow tracking, this data may be used to show personalised ads. If you decline, you will still see ads but they will be contextual (not personalised).

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

This app offers optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads and Power Pack). All transactions are processed securely by Apple. We do not receive, store, or process your payment details.

Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Game Center

This app uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Your Game Center alias and scores may be visible to other players. Game Center data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following locally on your device only (never transmitted to us):

  • High score and game progress
  • Daily challenge completion status
  • Purchase status (Remove Ads, Power Pack)
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial

Children

This app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured without child-directed treatment flags; if you are a parent and have concerns, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
